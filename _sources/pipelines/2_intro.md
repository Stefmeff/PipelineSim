# 2. Synchronous Pipelines

This chapter will focus on synchronous pipelines, which have become the standard approach for digital circuit and CPU design. Synchronous circuits update their state in coordination with a global `clock` signal. A clock signal is a logic signal that periodically oscillates between high and low values, providing a consistent pace at which tokens propagate through the pipeline stages. After an introduction to some conventional synchronous pipeline architectures {cite}`weste2011cmos,weste2011integrated`, this chapter also discusses possible optimization techniques like `time-borrowing` and `wave-pipelining`.
